About

Ethan Ramsey lives in Wiltshire, England, and has spent his career in the UK motor trade. He writes about work, money and freedom in a world being reshaped by AI, drawing on a lifelong interest in markets and how ordinary people get by. He also writes short fiction: cosy mysteries, quiet winter tales and warm, funny romances, each one built to be read in a single sitting. When he isn't writing, he's with his family, outdoors or in the kitchen.
