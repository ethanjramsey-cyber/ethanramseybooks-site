Who Gets to Live?

Work, Wealth and Freedom in the Age of AI

The machines could make enough for everyone. Who will get to share it?

Coming 29 November

Published 29 November 2026

About the book

Artificial intelligence can already write, analyse, design and advise. As machines take on more of the work that once paid for food, rent and a future, a world of extraordinary plenty is coming within reach. But plenty is not the same as access.

If employers no longer need our labour, what replaces the wage? Who will own the systems that do the work, and who will be left outside?

What the book explores

In Who Gets to Live?, Ethan Ramsey follows the story from the Luddite workshops of 1811 to the data centres of today and the possible futures beyond them. Drawing on the latest research and on vivid scenes from imagined lives in the decades ahead, he explores:

  • why AI could change tasks faster than it changes whole jobs, and why the first shock may be invisible
  • who is positioned to own the wealth automation creates, and how ordinary households could hold a stake
  • what abundance can and cannot fix: land, energy, housing and human attention
  • how governments would fund public services if wages stop carrying the tax system
  • the real evidence on universal basic income, from Finland and Kenya to the United States
  • what happens to money, debt and mortgages in a world without wages
  • where people find purpose when work becomes optional
  • how humanity keeps control of increasingly capable AI

Clear, balanced and urgent, Who Gets to Live? is for anyone who wants to understand the choices that will shape their children's lives, while there is still time to make them.
